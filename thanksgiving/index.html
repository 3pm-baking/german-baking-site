Good to Know

✿
Why frozen?

German cakes freeze beautifully, it keeps us from baking out of panic the night before, and it lets you stash dessert days early. Thawing instructions are provided on every label.

Can I order slices?

Thanksgiving is whole cakes only. Slices are always available at our farmers market stand.

What does pickup look like?

Pickup is at our market stand. After checkout you get an email with the exact stand details and window. Home delivery is quoted at checkout based on your address.
