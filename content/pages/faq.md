Short answer: 3pm German Baking is a German bakery in Asheville, NC. We
sell German cakes, pastries, and pantry goods at area farmers markets, take
direct orders with local delivery, and bake custom cakes for special
occasions. A few of our cakes are gluten-free; some products, like our
apple strudel, are vegan.

## Where to Find Us

### Where can I buy 3pm German Baking in Asheville?

Three ways: at local farmers markets, by direct order with local delivery
throughout the Asheville area, and as custom cakes ordered by email. See
[the market schedule on our homepage](/index.html#find-us) for this
season's days and times, or write to
[info@germanbakingasheville.com](mailto:info@germanbakingasheville.com) to arrange an order.

### Which markets do you attend?

We sell at farmers markets around Asheville and the surrounding towns, and
which market on which day changes with the season. The current schedule is
always on [our homepage](/index.html#find-us), and we also post updates on
[Instagram](https://instagram.com/3pmgermanbaking) when it moves.

### Do you deliver?

Yes. Direct orders can be delivered locally throughout the Asheville area.
Email [info@germanbakingasheville.com](mailto:info@germanbakingasheville.com) with what you would like and we will
arrange delivery time and details with you.

### What payment do you accept at the market?

Cash, card, Venmo, Apple Pay, and market tokens. No need to hit an ATM
before you stop by; bring what you have and we can take it from there.

### Can I order ahead instead of buying at the market?

Yes. Some products are available to preorder online, and anything else can
be arranged by email in advance of a market day. Ordering ahead is the
surest way to get exactly what you want, especially for whole cakes.

## Ordering and Custom Cakes

### Do you make custom cakes?

Yes. We bake custom cakes for birthdays, dinner parties, and special
occasions, and we also work with recurring private clients who want
authentic German baking regularly. Email
[info@germanbakingasheville.com](mailto:info@germanbakingasheville.com) or find us at a market to talk through what
you have in mind.

A couple of days is usually enough for most cakes, but the exact timing
depends on the upcoming market schedule, because market days set when we
bake. Reach out as early as you can and we will tell you honestly whether
your date works.

### Do you sell to coffee shops or restaurants?

Yes, we supply wholesale customers in the Asheville area. Email
[info@germanbakingasheville.com](mailto:info@germanbakingasheville.com) and we will send our current wholesale
list.

## Gluten-Free

### Are your cakes gluten-free?

Some are and some are not, and that is a choice we make on purpose. When a
recipe can be made gluten-free and still taste like itself, we make it
that way. When it cannot, like apple strudel, we leave it alone rather
than serve a version we are not proud of. Read the full story in
[why we do not make everything gluten-free](/blog/why-we-dont-make-everything-gluten-free.html).

### Which products are gluten-free?

Our Lemon Cake is gluten-free and dairy-free. German cheesecake, Donauwelle,
and our summer sheet cakes are gluten-free when the swap does not change
the taste, so ask at the market for the current lineup. Our spinach
tartlets use a gluten-free crust, and our pantry items, pudding powder and
vanilla sugar, are naturally gluten-free.

### Is the apple strudel gluten-free?

No. Real strudel dough must be rolled paper-thin, and gluten is what holds
it together, so we do not make a gluten-free strudel. We made it work for
another dietary need instead: our apple strudel is vegan, with a
dairy-free filling and gluten-free bread crumbs.

## Allergens

### Does the apple strudel contain eggs?

No. Our apple strudel is vegan, so it contains no eggs and no dairy. It
also uses gluten-free bread crumbs in the filling, though the dough itself
contains wheat gluten.

### Does anything contain nuts?

Several products do, including items with almonds, marzipan, or hazelnuts.
Each [product page](/products/) says what is inside. We bake in a shared
commercial kitchen, so cross-contact with other allergens is possible and
we cannot guarantee any product is completely free of traces. If you have
a serious allergy, please talk to us at the market or by email before
ordering; we would rather find you something safe than guess.

## The German Bit

### What is quark?

Quark is a fresh German dairy product somewhere between yogurt and cottage
cheese, and it is the base of a true German cheesecake. We looked hard for
it in the United States, which is why our cheesecake tastes the way it
does. More in [what is quark](/blog/what-is-quark.html).

### What is kaffee und kuchen?

It is the German tradition of coffee and cake in the afternoon, closer to
a daily institution than a weekend treat. Our whole menu is built for it.
Read more in [why 3pm kaffee und kuchen](/blog/why-3pm-kaffee-und-kuchen.html).

### What is the difference between strudel, streusel, and striezel?

Strudel is a rolled pastry stretched paper-thin. Streusel is a crumbly
butter-sugar topping baked over a cake. Striezel is a braided yeast bread,
like our Poppy Roll. We sell all three. The field guide:
[strudel vs. streusel vs. striezel](/blog/strudel-vs-streusel-vs-striezel.html).
