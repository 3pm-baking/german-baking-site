Almond Ricotta Cake

Saftiger Mandel Ricotta Kuchen

✿
Gluten-Free ✓

Moist almond and ricotta cake with orange zest — naturally gluten-free

Origin

This gluten-free almond ricotta cake combines the nutty richness of almond flour with the creamy texture of ricotta, brightened with orange zest. It's a naturally flourless cake that proves gluten-free baking can be every bit as satisfying as the original.

Perfect For

An excellent choice for those seeking naturally gluten-free desserts without compromise. Pairs beautifully with coffee or tea, and its moist, tender crumb makes it a standout at farmers markets and brunch tables.

Availability

Available at local Asheville-area farmers markets. Contact us for direct orders or catering inquiries.

Allergen Information

Contains: Dairy (ricotta), Eggs, Almonds (tree nuts). Naturally gluten-free.

Where to Buy

Almond Ricotta Cake is available through our wholesale program for coffee shops, markets, and restaurants throughout the Asheville, NC area. Learn more about wholesale partnerships or contact us directly.
