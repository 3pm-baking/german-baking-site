Seasonal Vegetable Quiche

Saisonale Gemüse-Quiche

✿
Gluten-Free ✓

Gluten-free savory quiche with seasonal vegetables and almond crust

Origin

This gluten-free quiche showcases the best of each season's harvest in a tender almond flour crust. Inspired by the German tradition of hearty savory tarts (herzhafte Quiches), it's a versatile option that changes with the farmer's market offerings throughout the year.

Perfect For

An ideal choice for lunch, brunch, or a light dinner. The almond crust provides a nutty complement to the seasonal filling, making it satisfying for both gluten-free and conventional eaters alike.

Availability

Available at local Asheville-area farmers markets. Seasonal availability — contact us for what's currently in rotation.

Allergen Information

Contains: Dairy, Eggs, Almonds (tree nuts). Naturally gluten-free.

Where to Buy

Seasonal Vegetable Quiche is available through our wholesale program for coffee shops, markets, and restaurants throughout the Asheville, NC area. Learn more about wholesale partnerships or contact us directly.
