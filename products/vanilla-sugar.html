What It Is

Vanillezucker is in nearly every German baking recipe—it is the subtle, fragrant backbone behind so many classic flavors. True German vanilla sugar is made by resting real vanilla beans in fine sugar, infusing it with a warm, floral aroma that vanilla extract alone cannot replicate. A small packet adds an unmistakably authentic character to everything from Kipferl to Käsekuchen. Naturally gluten-free and dairy-free.

How to Use

Use anywhere you would add vanilla extract or plain sugar in a baking recipe. Especially at home in Plätzchen (German Christmas cookies), whipped cream, Streusel toppings, and cake batters. One standard German packet (typically 8g) is equivalent to roughly one teaspoon of vanilla extract.
