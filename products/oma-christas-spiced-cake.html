Oma Christa's Spiced Cake

Gewürzkuchen

✿

A holiday chocolate cake right from Grandma's kitchen

Origin

A treasured family recipe from Oma Christa, this traditional German Gewürzkuchen brings together warming spices that have been beloved in German baking for generations. Cinnamon, cardamom, and cloves combine in a tender, fragrant cake that fills the kitchen with the unmistakable scent of a German Weihnachtsbäckerei—Christmas bakery—even in other seasons. A piece of this cake alongside an afternoon coffee is pure comfort.

Perfect For

Ideal for autumn and winter gatherings, holiday tables, and anyone who loves warming spice flavors. Also a distinctive offering for coffee shops looking to add something genuinely different to their pastry case. Gluten-free option available.

Availability

Available seasonally at local Asheville-area farmers markets. Contact us for direct orders or wholesale inquiries when in season.

Allergen Information

Contains: Dairy, Eggs, Wheat. Gluten-free option available on request.

Where to Buy

Oma Christa's Spiced Cake is available through our wholesale program for coffee shops, markets, and restaurants throughout the Asheville, NC area. Learn more about wholesale partnerships or contact us directly.
