Traditional German pudding mix for creating rich, creamy desserts at home. Simply combine with milk and sugar for an authentic German pudding experience.

What It Is

Puddingpulver is a pantry staple in German households—a simple starch-based powder that, when stirred into hot milk with a little sugar, produces a smooth, silky pudding with a clean, delicate flavor. Unlike instant pudding mixes, the German version is cooked on the stovetop and sets to a soft, spoonable consistency. Naturally gluten-free and dairy-free (when made with a plant-based milk).

How to Use

Combine one packet with cold milk (or oat/almond milk for a dairy-free version) and sugar, bring to a boil while stirring, and pour into cups or a mold to set. Serve warm or chilled. Also works beautifully as a filling layer in German layer cakes and tortes.
