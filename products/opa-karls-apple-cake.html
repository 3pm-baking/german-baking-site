Opa Karl's Apple Cake

Feiner Apfelkuchen

✿
Gluten-Free ✓ Dairy-Free ✓

A classic German apple cake from Apfelbauer Ernst-Walther Süss. Tender sponge topped with wreath-arranged apples and a shiny apricot glaze.

Origin

This Feiner Apfelkuchen comes from Apfelbauer Ernst-Walther Süss—an apple farmer in Germany whose orchard fruit defines the cake. Tender sponge, apples arranged in a wreath on top, and a shiny apricot glaze: the kind of straightforward, perfect apple cake that German bakery counters are known for. Opa Karl's name on it says everything about where the recipe lives in the family.

Perfect For

The Kaffee und Kuchen classic—afternoon coffee with a slice that tastes like a German orchard. Gluten-free without compromise, so everyone at the table gets the same cake. Also lovely slightly warm with a spoonful of plain yogurt or cream.

Availability

Available at local Asheville-area farmers markets. Contact us for direct orders, custom sizing for events, or wholesale inquiries.

Allergen Information

Contains: Eggs, Nuts (almond). Gluten-free.
