Delicious German cheese cake with pulls of chocolate swirls

Origin

Despite its name, this beloved cake is entirely a German creation. The "Russischer" (Russian) name is thought to be a nostalgic nod to Eastern European flavor influences in post-war German baking. The "Zupf" refers to the pulled or plucked chocolate dough that forms the signature crumbled topping—baked over a smooth quark cheesecake layer for a beautiful contrast of textures. It has been a staple of German bakeries and home kitchens for decades.

Perfect For

Anyone who loves both chocolate cake and cheesecake will find this irresistible. The dramatic two-tone appearance makes it a showstopper at markets and events. Gluten-free option available on request.
