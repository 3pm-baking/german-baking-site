Grandma Elbach's Lemon Cake

Origin

A gluten-free spin on a classic recipe from Oma Anneliese, this beloved family favorite has been cherished by generations. This traditional Zitronenkuchen brings bright citrus flavor with a tender, moist crumb—proof that gluten-free German baking can be just as delicious as the original.

Availability

Available at local Asheville-area farmers markets. Contact us for direct orders or wholesale inquiries.

Perfect For

Ideal for coffee shops and markets seeking gluten-free dessert options that don't compromise on authentic flavor. A crowd-pleaser for all dietary needs.
