Spinach-packed tartlets with gluten-free crust

Origin

A savory addition to our traditional German baking, these Spinat-Törtchen bring together fresh spinach and herbs in a buttery gluten-free crust. Savory tarts are a time-honored part of German café and market culture—a satisfying alternative to sweet pastries for those who prefer something more herbaceous and filling.

Perfect For

A standout item at farmers markets, brunch tables, and events. Ideal for guests who want something savory alongside coffee or tea. Naturally gluten-free—no substitutions needed—making them a reliable choice for mixed dietary groups.

Availability

Available at local Asheville-area farmers markets. Contact us for direct orders, event catering quantities, or wholesale inquiries.

Allergen Information

Contains: Dairy, Eggs. Naturally gluten-free. Contains spinach and herbs.

Where to Buy

Spinach Tartlets is available through our wholesale program for coffee shops, markets, and restaurants throughout the Asheville, NC area. Learn more about wholesale partnerships or contact us directly.
