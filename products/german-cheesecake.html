Light and creamy quark cheesecake with a tender crust—the authentic German classic.

Origin

A beloved classic from German café culture, Käsekuchen has been a centerpiece of afternoon coffee and cake—Kaffee und Kuchen—for generations. Unlike American cheesecake, the German version uses quark, a fresh dairy product, which gives it a lighter, creamier texture and a delicate tang. This recipe follows the traditional method Oma used, perfected over decades.

Perfect For

A crowd-pleaser at any gathering, afternoon coffee, birthday celebration, or farmers market. The familiar creaminess of cheesecake combined with its distinctly European character makes it approachable for all ages. Gluten-free option available—just ask at the market.
